Nazar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Nazar is a macOS menu bar app that watches public status pages and tells you when a service has an outage. This policy covers the Nazar app and this website, usenazar.com. Nazar is made by Zaahir Moolla.

The short version

  • The Nazar app does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
  • This website collects anonymous visit statistics, without cookies, to see how people use it.

The Nazar app

The app does not send us any personal data.

  • Status checks. Nazar requests each status page you add directly from that service. The service sees your IP address and the app version, like any web request. We do not see these requests.
  • On your Mac. Your list of services, your settings, and your notifications stay on your Mac. The optional "Find services from your installed apps" feature reads app names in your Applications folder and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Feedback. Feedback links open a pre-filled GitHub issue in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you submit it on GitHub.
  • Updates. The version downloaded from this website checks usenazar.com for new versions. The Mac App Store version leaves updates to the App Store.

This website (usenazar.com)

The website collects anonymous statistics about visits: which pages people view, which links and buttons they click, which site sent them, their country, and their browser and device type. We use this to see how people find Nazar and to fix problems with the site. It does not use cookies, and we do not use it to identify you.

We use two services for this:

  • Fathom Analytics counts visits and clicks.
  • PostHog counts visits and clicks, and reports errors on the site.

Like any website, the servers that deliver the site see your IP address. Your theme choice is saved in your browser only.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Children

Nazar is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. The full history is public in the Nazar GitHub repository.
